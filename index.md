# PokerOdds Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 6, 2026*

This policy explains what data the PokerOdds app for iOS and Android ("the app") handles. The app is provided by Samad Pour, Kurvenstrasse 1, 9062 Lustmühle, Switzerland ("we"), who is responsible for the data described here.

## The short version

- There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name or email address.
- The cards you enter and the odds you see stay on your device.
- The app has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
- The only data that leaves your device relates to in-app purchases, and it is handled by our purchase provider, RevenueCat.

## Delete your data

To ask us to delete your PokerOdds data:

1. Email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS] with the subject "PokerOdds data deletion".
2. Include your Google Play order number or Apple receipt, so we can find your anonymous record.
3. We'll confirm when your data has been deleted, within 30 days.

**What we delete:** the anonymous ID, purchase information and device details held by RevenueCat for you.

**What we keep:** only the purchase records the law requires us to keep, such as for tax and accounting, for as long as that law requires. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Deleting this data doesn't cancel what you bought, because your purchase belongs to your Apple or Google account. If you use the app or restore your purchase afterwards, a new anonymous record is created.

## Data we process

**Purchase data.** To sell PokerOdds Pro, restore it, and recognize customers who bought the app when it was paid, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes on our behalf:

- a random, anonymous ID created by the app, which is not linked to your name or email
- your purchase information, including the Apple receipt or Google purchase token, and when you last used the app
- technical details such as your device type and operating system

As with any internet service, your IP address may be visible to the servers the app connects to.

**Payments.** Apple and Google process all payments. We never see your payment card or bank details. Their own privacy policies apply to those payments.

## Why we use this data

We use purchase data to provide what you bought, to restore purchases, to help prevent fraud, and to see how purchases perform overall. For users in the EU, EEA and UK, our legal bases are performing our contract with you and our legitimate interest in running and improving the app and preventing fraud.

## Who receives this data

We share this data only with:

- **RevenueCat, Inc.**, which acts as our data processor and stores data in the United States under contractual safeguards. See the [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
- **Apple** or **Google**, depending on where you got the app. See the [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We don't sell your data.

## How long we keep data

Purchase data is kept for as long as it's needed to provide your purchases and to meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules, and to resolve disputes.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to receive a copy of it. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email us at the address below. Because the data is anonymous, we may need information from you to find it, such as a purchase receipt.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age set by your country, and we don't knowingly collect children's personal data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
